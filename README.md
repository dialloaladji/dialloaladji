## 🚀 Featured Product Case Study

### 🔍 [Product Information Retrieval](https://github.com/dialloaladji/product-information-retrieval)

An AI-powered product data enrichment solution designed for industrial and B2B distribution contexts.

The goal is to help product data, category management and e-commerce teams enrich incomplete product information using external search, LLM-based extraction, structured data validation and observability.

**Product problem:**
Industrial distributors often manage incomplete or inconsistent product data, including missing GTINs, manufacturer part numbers, technical specifications, categories and normalized descriptions.

**Target users:**
Product data teams, category managers, procurement teams, e-commerce teams and sales enablement teams.

**Business value:**
Reduce manual enrichment work, improve product discoverability, increase product data quality and support better commercial decisions.

**Key product metrics:**

* Enrichment coverage rate
* Matching confidence score
* Human validation rate
* Processing time per product
* Cost per enrichment
* Data quality improvement rate

**Technical scope:**
FastAPI · Llama 3.1 via Groq · Tavily · PostgreSQL/SQLite · Langfuse · Docker · GitHub Actions CI/CD
